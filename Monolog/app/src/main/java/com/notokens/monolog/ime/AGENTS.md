# Quick Shortcuts

Preserve the approved Quick Shortcuts design unless the user explicitly asks to change it. Keep the right-aligned glass callout, the Reminder, Alarm, and Journal tiles, and the visible Save a note action. The callout floats above the keyboard without a full-width background or extra keyboard height. Keep the M control in the keyboard row and avoid adding a floating M button.

Keep shortcut hold duration and haptics user-configurable in Settings. Outside taps dismiss the menu; tapping M while it is open only dismisses it and must never also capture.

Holding M is the only way to open Quick Shortcuts from the keyboard.
