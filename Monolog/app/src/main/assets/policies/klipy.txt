KLIPY media and ads

GIFs and stickers are optional online services. If you enable them, Monolog requests
catalog items when you open either tab and sends media searches you choose to submit.
KLIPY and its media delivery providers may receive those search terms, your network
address, and a locale. You can turn the feature off under Keyboard & shortcuts.

The KLIPY catalog may contain sponsored items. Any future sponsored items shown in
Monolog will be labeled. When advertising is active, KLIPY and its advertising
partners may process device, network, and interaction information to display and
measure ads. See the provider's privacy policy and applicable terms for details.

The media feature does not receive ordinary keyboard drafts, your clipboard, your
journal, or your voice recordings. Its online tabs are unavailable in incognito
and protected fields. Media selected for insertion is sent to the app you are typing in.
